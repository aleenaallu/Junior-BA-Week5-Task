# Junior-BA-Week5-Task
## Week 5 Task: Business Strategy Development and Recommendation Report 

Objective: 
To exercise your strategic thinking skills by developing a business strategy based on an analysis of available market data. This task combines data-driven insights with strategic planning to propose actionable business recommendations. 


Key Steps: 
1. Begin with a situational analysis using publicly available data sources.
2. Perform a SWOT analysis on the chosen business case.
3. Develop and articulate a clear business strategy aimed at addressing identified challenges and leveraging market opportunities.
4. Support your recommendations with data insights, charts, and trend analyses.
5. Include a discussion on potential risks and mitigation strategies.
